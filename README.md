## Hey there, I'm Javi! 👋

### About me 👨‍💻

I am a freelancing robotics engineer with extensive experience turning prototypes into operational products. I like being involved from the early research and development until well into the release and operational stage of the products.

My specialization focuses in real-time perception and AI, although I also have numerous contributions throughout the whole robotics stack. Consequently, my work has yielded patent applications, open source contributions and conference publications. Check my [website](https://jamacias.github.io).

### Services 🤖

* Industrialization of robotics systems.
* Development of perception and computer vision solutions.
* Robotics software architecture and development.
* Sensor selection, calibration and integration.
* Robust robotics design and error management.
* Control strategy and motion planning.
* Simulation development.
* Feel free to [contact me](mailto:jamaciastf@gmail.com) to discuss custom projects.
